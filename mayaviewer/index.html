Setup

How it works

Download the plugin

Download the free Maya Viewer Python plugin from this page.

Copy to Maya scripts

Copy the plugin into your Autodesk Maya scripts folder.

Run the server

In Maya, run the plugin/server from the script editor or shelf setup.

Open Maya Viewer

Launch Maya Viewer on your iPhone or iPad.

Enter connection details

Enter the Maya computer IP address, port, and optional PIN.

Connect and review

Tap Connect and start reviewing the scene on your device.

import rfx_mayaviewer as mv
mv.start_server(pin="optional-pin")
Requirements

What you need

  • Autodesk Maya required on the desktop machine.
  • iPhone or iPad required for the iOS app.
  • Maya computer and iPhone should be on the same local network for normal use.
  • Optional PIN is available for basic connection protection.
  • Remote access over different WiFi/mobile data is not officially supported unless the user has their own secure network/VPN setup.
  • Maya Viewer is a scene preview and AR review tool, not an Arnold renderer replacement.

Privacy Policy

Maya Viewer Privacy Policy

Maya Viewer is designed as a local companion tool for Autodesk Maya. It does not collect personal data and does not send scene data to Reimagine Fx.

  • Maya Viewer does not collect personal data.
  • Maya Viewer does not use analytics, ads, or tracking.
  • Camera access is used only for AR mode and remains local on the device.
  • Local Network access is used only to connect the iPhone/iPad to the user’s own Maya session.
  • Server IP address, port, and optional PIN may be stored locally on the device for convenience.
  • Scene data, geometry, materials, textures, cameras, lights, and transforms are transferred only between the user’s Maya session and the user’s device.
  • Reimagine Fx does not receive, store, or process the user’s Maya scene data.
  • No personal data is sold or shared.

About

Built by Reimagine Fx

Maya Viewer was built by Amar Shinde at Reimagine Fx to help 3D artists review Maya scenes faster, cleaner, and closer to the way they actually work. It is designed as a companion to Autodesk Maya, giving artists a lightweight way to inspect scenes, check assets, preview cameras, review materials, and experience their work in AR without leaving their creative workflow.

FAQ

Is the Maya plugin free?

Yes, the Maya Python plugin is free to download from this page.

Does Maya Viewer render with Arnold?

No. Maya Viewer previews scene data on iPhone and in AR. It is not a replacement for Arnold rendering.

Can multiple devices connect to one Maya session?

Yes, multiple devices can connect when they are on the same reachable local network.

Can I connect over mobile data or a different WiFi network?

Not officially. For normal use, Maya Viewer is designed for local network connections. Remote use should only be attempted through a secure VPN or private network setup.

Does the app collect my scene files?

No. Scene data moves only between your Maya session and your device. Reimagine Fx does not receive or store your files.

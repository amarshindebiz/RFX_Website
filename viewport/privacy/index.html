Privacy Policy

Viewport Privacy Policy

Viewport is an on-device 3D model viewer for iOS. It does not collect personal data, has no accounts, and never uploads the files you open to Reimagine Fx or anyone else.

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Back to Viewport

Privacy Policy Details

  • Viewport does not collect personal data.
  • Viewport does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • All 3D loading, conversion and rendering happen entirely on your device.
  • Files you open, and their textures and materials, are processed locally and are never uploaded. Reimagine Fx does not receive, store, or process them.
  • Camera access is requested only for AR mode (AR Quick Look) and remains local on the device.
  • A list of your recently opened files, generated thumbnails, and your viewer preferences are stored locally on the device for convenience. You can remove recent items at any time inside the app.
  • Viewport has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • No personal data is sold or shared.
  • When you choose to share a screenshot, model file, or stats, that action is handled by the standard iOS share sheet and goes only to the destination you pick.

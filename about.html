With 8+ years in the visual effects industry, I've built a career around one obsession: making the impossible look inevitable on screen. I'm a 3D Compositor, FX Artist, and Motion Designer who lives at the intersection of technical precision and cinematic storytelling.

My toolkit spans the full spectrum of professional VFX — from simulating destruction in Houdini to building photorealistic worlds in Unreal Engine, compositing seamless shots in Nuke, and crafting fluid motion in After Effects. Every tool is a means to one end: transporting audiences to worlds that don't exist yet.

Beyond client work, I'm deeply invested in the next generation of VFX artists. Through YouTube, Skillshare, and my Gumroad store, I share the actual workflows, assets, and techniques I use on real productions — no fluff, just what works.

Blender Viewer

Review Blender scenes live on iPhone, iPad and in AR.

Blender Viewer is a focused companion for artists who want their Blender scene within reach away from the desktop. A lightweight Blender add-on streams geometry, materials, textures, cameras, lights and real scene statistics over your local network to a professional mobile viewport — with inspection tools, object transforms and AR placement built in.

Getting Started

How it works

Install the add-on

Download the package for Blender 4.5 LTS or Blender 5.x below and install the ZIP directly.

Start the server

Open Blender Viewer in the 3D View sidebar and start the local server. IP address and QR code visibility can be controlled independently.

Use one network

Keep Blender and your iPhone on the same trusted local Wi-Fi network.

Connect the app

Scan the panel QR code or enter the displayed IP address and port.

Snap the scene

Request the current scene, then use Live Sync to receive supported updates.

Review anywhere

Orbit, inspect, transform, switch cameras and place the scene in AR.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Does Blender Viewer open .blend files on iPhone?

No. The Blender add-on reads the open desktop scene and streams supported review data to the app. Blender remains the source of truth.

Does it work away from my computer?

Blender Viewer is designed for a direct local-network connection to a running Blender session. Remote access is not currently an official workflow.

Are materials and textures supported?

Yes. The viewer supports key Principled PBR values and connected color, metallic, roughness and normal textures that the add-on can resolve.

Can I edit my Blender scene from iPhone?

You can select supported objects and use Move, Rotate and Scale gizmos. Completed transform gestures create Blender undo history, with Undo and Redo available in the viewer.

Can I use it without Blender?

An included offline demo lets you explore the viewer. Live scene review requires the Blender add-on and a running Blender session.

Is the iOS app available?

Yes — Blender Scene Viewer is live on the App Store. The free Blender add-on for desktop is available here, and the iOS app is the companion viewer for your iPhone or iPad.

Privacy

Private by design

  • Blender Viewer does not collect personal data.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.
  • Scene data is transferred only between your Blender session and your device.
  • Camera access is used only for QR connection scanning and AR mode.
  • Connection history and preferences are stored locally for convenience.
  • No accounts and no cloud scene upload.

The Problem

Messy Maya scenes slow down every department.

Production scenes accumulate geometry, cameras, lights, rig controls, caches, helper locators, curves, Bifrost nodes, image planes, and unsorted technical data. The Outliner becomes a search problem before the actual work even starts.

RFX Smart Organizer gives artists a compact Maya panel for scanning, reviewing, sorting, renaming, analyzing, and exporting scene health information before handoff.

Core Features

Built for artists who need scenes to stay readable.

Practical scene structure tools, not generic cleanup. Designed around Maya Display Layers, Outliner Groups, naming workflows, and scene hygiene checks.

Smart Scene Organizer

Scan Maya DAG content and classify scene objects into production-friendly categories like Geometry, Lights, Cameras, Curves, Locators, Volumes, Bifrost, nDynamics, and Unsorted.

Layers + Outliner Groups

FAQ

Questions before installing?

What version of Maya is supported? +

RFX Smart Organizer supports Autodesk Maya 2025 or newer.

Does it require Arnold? +

No. The organizer itself does not require Arnold. Some Scene Intel checks may mention Arnold-specific lighting guidance when relevant, but the core organizer works without a renderer dependency.

Does Scan Scene modify my scene? +

No. Scan Scene only reads the scene and updates the UI.

Can I organize only selected objects? +

Yes. Use Sort Selected when you want to organize only part of the scene.

Can I create my own categories? +

Yes. Use Settings > Add Custom Category. You can match by Maya node types, name patterns, or both.

Can I export a report? +

Yes. Scene Intel includes an Export Report option that you can share with your team.
